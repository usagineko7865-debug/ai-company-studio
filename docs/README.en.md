# AI Company Studio

A local web app where you, a ChatGPT-based CEO, and a Claude-based CTO build a business together. Its defining goal is to turn a proven operating method into a reproducible “company template” that its creator can sell to others. Phase 1 provides the business-building and evidence foundation for that marketplace.

- Switch between a free mock mode and real AI
- Save conversations as business ideas
- Save proof, repeatable steps, and failure conditions as a portable company template
- Guard API spending before each request
- Available in Japanese, English, Simplified Chinese, Brazilian Portuguese, and Spanish

On Windows, download the Windows ZIP from Releases and double-click `AI-Company-Studio.exe`; Python is not required. Phase 1 is for demand validation and never publishes, sells, or purchases anything automatically. Subscriptions and revenue management for the paid edition are planned through Stripe.
