# AI Company Studio

A local web app where you, a ChatGPT-based CEO, and a Claude-based CTO develop a business in a group-chat style workspace.

- Switch between a free mock mode and real AI
- Save conversations as business ideas
- Guard API spending before each request
- Available in Japanese, English, Simplified Chinese, Brazilian Portuguese, and Spanish

On Windows, double-click `AI会議を開く.bat`. Phase 1 is for demand validation and never publishes, sells, or purchases anything automatically. Subscriptions and revenue management for the paid edition are planned through Stripe.
